# AMLD+0 Eureka Lab

## Free Public Research Beta

Welcome to the AMLD+0 Eureka Lab.

AMLD+0 is an experimental, verifier-gated mathematical research architecture for investigating conjectures, searching for proofs and refutations, and discovering useful intermediate mathematical results.

**Our mission:** Make experimental mathematical conjecture investigation accessible to researchers, students, and independent mathematicians.

### What can I submit?

- Mathematical conjectures
- Formal logic and theorem-proving problems
- Questions about consequences of mathematical hypotheses
- Problems suitable for Eureka-style discovery

### How it works

1. Submit a precisely stated conjecture with its assumptions.
2. Selected submissions are investigated under a defined research budget.
3. Findings are checked against the applicable verifier.
4. Receive a report identifying verified results, provisional discoveries, or unresolved questions.

Proofs and discoveries are not guaranteed. An unsuccessful search does not establish that a conjecture is false or unprovable.

### Free Public Access

Our goal is to offer an extended period of free public research access, initially subject to submission and resource limits.

### Submit a Conjecture

Visit our GitHub Discussions area to propose a conjecture for public consideration.

Do not submit confidential or unpublished material publicly. Private submission arrangements will be announced separately.
